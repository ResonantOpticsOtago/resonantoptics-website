title: Julius von Hasst Fellowship awarded to Gerd Leuchs

We have been awarded $150,000 to host the Julius von Haast Fellow [Prof. Gerd Leuchs](https://www.mpl.mpg.de/divisions/leuchs-division/?L=0) in our group. He will be visiting us for one month for the next three years! The first visit will be in December 2017 to give a keynote at ANZCOP2017 in Queenstown.

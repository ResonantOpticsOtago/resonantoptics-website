Today our article on generating a *Octave-spanning tunable parametric oscillation in crystalline Kerr microresonators* has been published in [**Nature Photonics**](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41566-019-0485-4). You can also find the abstract [here](../../publication/sayson-octave-spanning-2019/) or the read only link from [Nature Photonics](https://rdcu.be/bJgEZ).

This work is a close collaboration between our group at the University of Otago and the group of Stuart Murdoch and Miro Erkintalo at the [University of Auckland](https://laserlab.blogs.auckland.ac.nz/).

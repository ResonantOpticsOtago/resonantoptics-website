We have been awarded $150,000 from the Royal Society of New Zealand [Catalyst:Leader](https://www.royalsociety.org.nz/what-we-do/funds-and-opportunities/catalyst-fund/catalyst-leaders/recipients/) funding to host Dr Dmitry Strekalov. He will be visiting us for one month each year for the next three years! The first visit will be in February 2021 (COVID depending).

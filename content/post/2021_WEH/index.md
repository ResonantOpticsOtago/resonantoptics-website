We are excited that our WE-Heraeus Seminar #749 [*Photonic Links for Quantum Technology Platforms*](https://www.we-heraeus-stiftung.de/veranstaltungen/seminare/2021/photonic-links-for-quantum-technology-platforms/main/) starts in two weeks. 

Two years ago Nick, Harald and [Johannes](https://quantumids.com/)  applied for funding from the [WE-Heraeus foundation](https://www.we-heraeus-stiftung.de/) to host a Seminar in the wonderful halls of the [Physikzentrum in Bad Honnef, Germany](https://www.dpg-physik.de/ueber-uns/physikzentrum-bad-honnef/index_html?set_language=en).

Unfortunately this year we will only meet on Meet Anyway platform. We are looking forward to an exciting program.

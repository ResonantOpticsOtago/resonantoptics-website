Mika's work on *Multichannel upconversion of terahertz radiation in an optical disk resonator* has been published in [**Optics Express**](https://opg.optica.org/OE/abstract.cfm?uri=OE-33-5-10302) and selected as **Editor's Pick**. 

The research presented in the paper detailed our results on the upconversion of six THz channels with MHz bandwidth, centered around 0.157THz, and separated by about 10 Hz. The work was funded by our Smart Ideas 2021 grant and the Agnes-Blackie fellowshop from Te Whai Ao Dodd-Walls Centre.

 
You can also find the abstract [here](../../publication/suresh-multichannel-2025).

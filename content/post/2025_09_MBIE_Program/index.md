{{< figure src="HaraldMikaAnnika.jpeg" title="Prof. Harald Schwefel, Agnes Blaikie Fellow Dr. Mallika Suresh and A/Prof. Annika Seppälä in front of the fs-laser used to fabricate ultra high quality optical resonators" >}}

We have been awarded $10,525,544 (excl GST) for *Building critical capability for space-based climate monitoring
with next generation photonics* within the [MBIE Endeavour Program](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/science-and-technology/science-and-innovation/funding-information-and-opportunities/investment-funds/endeavour-fund/research-programmes-successful-proposals) scheme! 

The project team is led out of the University of Otago, with key individuals placed at the Earth Science New Zealand (formerly NIWA/MetService), Paihau-Robinson Research Institute, and the Universities of Auckland, Canterbury, and Waikato. Key international partnerships include NASA/JPL, the Finnish Meteorological Institute, ANCDF Canberra, UC3MMadrid, and Colorado School of Mines, USA. Key industry partners are Kea Aerospace, Whitika-LuxFidelity and Shamrock Industries. Community engagement will be led by Tūhura Otago Museum and Aerospace NZ.
